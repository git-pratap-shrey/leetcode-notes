**Brute Force:**

**Optimal Approach:**
Apply the **Triangle Angle Sum Theorem**, which states that the sum of all interior angles in a Euclidean triangle is always $180^\circ$.
*   **Logic:** $\text{Third Angle} = 180^\circ - (\text{Angle}_1 + \text{Angle}_2)$
*   **Time Complexity:** $O(1)$
*   **Space Complexity:** $O(1)$

**The 'Aha' Moment:**
The specific mention of "Triangle" and "Angles" is a direct signal to use the $180^\circ$ constant.
